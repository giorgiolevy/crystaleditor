Plane & buckling

Anchor a plane on an atom and inspect how far the atoms of a site sit off it. "Flatten" projects the selected site's atoms onto the plane, removing the buckling.

Measure

Turn on Measure, then click atoms in the view. Two atoms give the distance between them; three or more give a chain of distances.
